The Council of Dans

Dan Borges are scattered across cities, industries, and countries. The council brings them together to create, share, and empower each other. If that's your name, you're invited.

Request to join

One name, many talents

Every member shares a name and a reputation. The council puts that in common so each Dan can lean on the skills, reach, and ideas of the others.

Solve problems together

Bring a hard personal, professional, or business problem to the council. Other Dans offer expertise, introductions, research, or solutions, like a multidisciplinary advisory board that answers to your name.

Refer work worldwide

If one Dan owns a business and another has the right clients, they send work each other's way. Opportunities move across cities, industries, and countries. We have a shared reputation to uphold, after all.

Build things as a team

Form project teams to make art, write software, develop products, back small ventures, or start joint businesses. Participation and ownership follow what each Dan actually contributes.
